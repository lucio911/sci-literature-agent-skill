# Evidence and citation quality gates

## Metadata vs evidence

- **Crossref verified** means the DOI resolves and the returned metadata was inspected. It does **not** prove SCI/SCIE indexed, peer review quality, absence of corrections/retractions, or support for any statement.
- **Full-text checked** means the actual lawful-access manuscript was inspected and a specific figure, table, section or page can be located.
- **Abstract checked** may support broad qualitative claims explicitly stated in the abstract, not granular numbers or interpretation of methods never described.
- If only title/DOI/metadata are available, do **not** assert `support=direct` and do not insert the paper as evidence.

## Methodological matching for geotechnical papers

For each candidate record: model/prototype scale, constitutive law, soil state and drainage, loading application and sequence, crack orientation, initial crack depth, XFEM enrichment criteria, validation basis, and output metrics. Do not say two studies are in agreement simply because their broad subjects overlap.

## Source priority

1. Primary peer-reviewed research directly about the claim.
2. Original methods papers and seminal foundational theory for methodology background.
3. Authoritative reviews for synthesized overview only.
4. Standards, datasets and official publisher records for specific normative/metadata assertions.
5. Preprints, theses, repository manuscripts: identify status explicitly; avoid presenting as peer-reviewed journal evidence.

## Review record template

| Claim ID | Exact claim | Bib key | DOI | Source basis | Locator | Supported finding | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Exact sentence | Author2024Title | Verified DOI | full_text | Section 3, Fig. 5 | What was actually observed | direct/qualified/contradicts |

Do not fabricate a source locator. Distinguish missing evidence from disproven statements.

## Retraction and correction risks

When stakes warrant it, check publisher article page, Crossmark/Retraction Watch or authoritative notices separately. Metadata flags are not complete and absence of a flag is not evidence of good standing.
